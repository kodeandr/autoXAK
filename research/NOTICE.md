# VED attribution

The fuel models and aggregate experimental reports were produced by the AutoXAK
project using the Vehicle Energy Dataset (VED), https://github.com/gsoh/VED.

VED copyright 2019 Geunseob (GS) Oh. The upstream repository distributes its
material under Apache License 2.0; a copy is in `docs/licenses/VED-APACHE-2.0.txt`.
The raw VED archives and individual journey traces are not redistributed here.

The AutoXAK preparation, feature extraction, physics calibration and trained
CatBoost weights are new experimental work. These weights are not published or
endorsed by the VED authors. Dataset file hashes and model provenance are in
`data_manifest.json` and `app/ml/artifacts/provenance.json`.
